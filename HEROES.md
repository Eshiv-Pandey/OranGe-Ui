# Hero collection

Four new original Hero layouts precede the existing editorial and product openings: Good company, Oddkin, Afterimage and After hours.

Good company interprets the purple oversized typography reference with original character accents and configurable navigation. Oddkin interprets the lime illustrated reference with heavy condensed type, a pink action and oRangeUi's own curious characters. Afterimage uses an original procedural orange, pink and midnight woven glow. After hours uses the existing Neon 404 sign's orange glass tubes, mint edge and action, and cream typography on black.

Branding, headings, descriptions, both action destinations and short closing notes are configurable. Afterimage accepts replacement artwork. After hours accepts sign text and a flicker toggle. All sign sound is off; flicker respects reduced motion, page visibility and viewport visibility.

The supplied references were inspected through Pinterest's public pin image endpoint. They guided the compositions and materials, but no reference pixels, brands, slogans or characters are shipped. The gradient is an original mathematical glow field with independently seeded warp-and-weft ink patterns. The procedural source and reference captures remain local under out/section-expansion/. Public asset and OFL font notices accompany downloads.

Source ZIPs and registry payloads include complete standalone React source, styles, fonts and artwork. Registry files embed public assets as data URLs.
