# Original section material

`woven-glow.webp` is original procedural artwork by oRangeUi, released under the repository's MIT license. It was authored on 12 November 2026 using a seeded trigonometric glow field, an original orange and midnight palette, and warped warp-and-weft ink patterns. No third-party reference pixels were sampled or copied. The image includes origin metadata.

The playful character and sticker plates used by the Bento and Hero layouts are original oRangeUi assets from `public/art/footer/`. Keep that folder's artwork notice when redistributing those illustrations. Font licenses are under `public/fonts/sections/`.
