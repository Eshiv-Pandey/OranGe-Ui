# Original footer artwork

These landscape plates, geometric characters and sticker ornaments were authored for oRangeUi. They contain no pixels, logos or characters copied from the supplied visual references.

The source scripts are `tools/generate-footer-landscapes.py` and `tools/generate-footer-playful-art.py`. Blue Hour's subtle print texture is also generated locally from a fixed random seed, without reference pixels. The generated artwork is distributed under the project's MIT license. Keep the project license and this notice with redistributed assets.

Typography uses Cormorant Garamond by Christian Thalmann and Pacifico by Vernon Adams, Jacques Le Bailly, Botjo Nikoltchev and Ani Petrova, both under the SIL Open Font License. Their complete notices are bundled under `public/fonts/footer/`.
