# Portfolio artwork

The transparent truck-art.webp and favicon-red.png are copied unchanged from Eshiv Pandey's current portfolio source, with the owner's authorization for this library. Keep this credit with redistributed artwork. Supply your own artwork through imageSrc, truckImageSrc and faviconSrc when adapting the components.

The component tree, nebula shader, marigold petal drawing, monument SVG, irregular sticker geometry and animation settings are ported from the live portfolio components. Pinterest references are used to inspect interactions and materials, and are not shipping assets.

Geist and Pixelify Sans use the library's existing licensed font files. Doto, Geist Mono and Noto Sans Devanagari are self-hosted in public/fonts/portfolio/ with their complete SIL Open Font License notices. Font outlines are preserved while converting the downloaded TrueType files to WOFF2.
