# Neon, chai, pavilion and film components

The site 404 route uses `ChaiBreak404`. `NeonSign404`, `NeonNotFound`, `ChaiPour`, `PavilionFooter` and `EndlessFilm` are independently reusable and their docs include interactive previews and source downloads.

```tsx
import { NeonSign404 } from "./components/ui/neon-sign-404";
import { ChaiBreak404 } from "./components/ui/chai-break-404";
import { PavilionFooter } from "./components/ui/pavilion-footer";
import { EndlessFilm } from "./components/ui/endless-film";

<NeonSign404 glow={0.65} flickerInterval={[4000, 9000]} />
<NeonSign404 digits="OPEN" color="#ff65ad" borderColor="#58bcff" pixelated />
<ChaiBreak404 speed={0.8} grain={0.025} />
<PavilionFooter pixelated />
<EndlessFilm label="Let the good days roll" speed={34} />
```

Neon `glow` changes bloom opacity between 0 and 1 without weakening the glass core. `flickerInterval` sets the random delay between sequences in milliseconds. `useNeonFlicker` returns unlit indices; pass an element ref for offscreen pausing.

The neon playground shows one complete recovery scene. Its hanging sign uses broad orange faces, warm outlined tubes, curled glass ends, a teal edge and light spilling onto brick. Component sizing remains available through props; the playground does not show size samples. Pixel tubes and Pixel pavilion remain optional inspector controls.

ChaiBreak404 fills its page with cream, a brass kettle, block-print sleeves, bangles, marigold toran and floral borders. Both Hindi chai labels remain removed. Its recovery copy and links remain HTML above the full-stage SVG. ChaiPour exposes an optional `fullscreen` prop; ChaiBreak404 always enables it. A resize observer matches the SVG to the actual page aspect ratio; sleeves extend off the upper-left and lower-right corners, keeping the mobile recovery-copy region clear. After the pour, three independent steam glyphs form 404 at 108-unit spacing. Unclipped blur, rounded white strokes, cloud lobes and a warm outline preserve the steam material and visible digit gaps.

Chai `speed` scales the preparation, pour and return; smaller values pour more slowly. The full glass retains its volume after this sequence. `grain` controls SVG noise opacity, capped at 0.15. Colors are scoped CSS variables documented on the component pages. Sound is procedural, optional and disabled by default. Motion pauses in hidden tabs or offscreen; reduced motion shows a still full glass and steam-cloud 404.

Chai releases only after the kettle tilts at least 28 degrees. Its leading edge travels from the spout to the rim over 420ms, then through the glass over 140ms before adding volume. After cutoff, liquid already in flight reaches the glass and the full glass stays filled. The descending jet uses rendered SVG transformation matrices to meet the rim after responsive placement or kettle rotation; the interior stream stays clipped inside the glass and ends at the animated tea and foam surface. Reduced motion hides both streams. Dragging changes the kettle angle, with its gripping hand attached to the kettle; releasing eases it back into the sequence. No tilt slider is shown. The toran uses layered marigold petals, hanging leaves and tassels inside the page's upper edge.

The kettle and hand fit the vertical space above the rim on short, wide pages, keeping the grip visible and preserving a downward pour. The sleeve reaches the upper-left edge through the full motion. Hand artwork is split around the kettle handle and glass: far fingers and palm are behind the objects, while curled fingertips and the opposing thumb are in front. Smaller nails, soft skin shading and restrained joint creases replace the earlier heavy finger outlines. The recovery copy remains clear of the kettle.

PavilionFooter uses a symmetric central pavilion with floral dome carving, balcony supports, ornamental columns and steps. Three smaller neighboring buildings use different softer blues. The Light the pavilion button visibly warms the arches of all four buildings to amber; Dim the pavilion returns them to the sky color. The root's `data-lit` state sets inherited `--pavilion-window` and `--pavilion-window-glow` values, which reach the architecture inside each SVG `use` instance. Fill and glow fade over 700ms. Four birds follow separate automatic paths with articulated wings, bodies, tails and intermittent glides; there is no flight control. Motion pauses in hidden tabs or offscreen. Pointer parallax eases toward the cursor and returns to center on leave. Reduced motion rests the birds and removes parallax; navigation and lighting remain usable without transitions. Back to top keeps its text and arrow aligned, scrolls to the beginning and focuses the heading.

EndlessFilm uses one continuous, lit photographic ribbon on a pure black (`#000`) background in both host palettes. Its light foreground and visible focus edge remain readable independently of the host theme. Photographs, borders and punched sprockets share an atlas registered to distance along a spatial mesh. A matte front material (roughness 0.94, metalness 0, no clearcoat), gentler lighting, shallow twist and fewer obscuring folds keep photographs clear. Perspective, surface normals, a darker reverse face, depth-tested overlap and soft offset shadows retain spatial depth. Sprockets remain transparent openings in the photographic texture and its shadow depth material.

Wheel input over the film and scrolling its surrounding page or preview feed the same photograph and sprocket phase in both directions. Dragging scrubs the strip; vertical touch scroll remains available through `touch-action: pan-y`, and horizontal touch movement scrubs it. Previous, Next, Pause film, Reverse and the keyboard-accessible photograph picker remain available. Selecting a photograph pauses automatic feed and announces its caption through the status region. External `paused`, keyboard focus and reduced motion stop automatic movement; manual navigation remains usable. Hidden-tab and offscreen scheduling pause decorative motion.

The renderer loads Three.js lazily, supports DPR 2 within a 2,500,000-pixel backing-image budget, and caps automatic painting at 30 frames per second. Each shared photograph-atlas cell is rasterized at up to 640 by 500 pixels, bounded by the device's maximum texture size. This keeps photographs and sprockets sharp on high-DPI screens. Unavailable or lost WebGL shows the photograph fallback while keeping captions, controls and the picker; context restoration restores the ribbon. Unmount aborts renderer initialization, removes wheel, scroll and context listeners, disconnects the resize observer and releases geometry, materials, textures, shadow maps and the WebGL renderer. Empty photo collections show an empty state.

WallSwitch uses a lazy Three.js solid model for the polished appearance, with a beveled plastic plate, recessed rocker and cast shadows. The Radix switch retains labels, focus, keyboard, controlled state and native form support. CSS supplies the fallback for unavailable WebGL or a lost context and for other appearances. Its portable bundle includes the renderer, Three.js and Three.js types. Resources and observers are released on unmount.

Neon, chai and pavilion artwork is original inline SVG and CSS. The wall switch and film ribbon use original solid geometry. Reference images are not distributed. ChaiYatra remains a component CSS alias for the existing SIL OFL licensed Yatra One asset at `public/fonts/desi/yatra-one.woff2`, used by the recovery heading after removal of the Hindi labels. FilmCormorant is the component CSS alias for self-hosted Cormorant Garamond italic, weight 500, at `public/fonts/film/cormorant-garamond-italic-latin.woff2`. Its source and SIL OFL notice are recorded in `public/fonts/film/NOTICE.md` and `public/fonts/film/OFL-cormorant-garamond.txt`. Film photographs retain their existing provenance in `public/photos/keepsakes/NOTICE.md`; portable source downloads retain the photo and font notices. These aliases, artwork palettes and local lettering sizes remain scoped to the components and inherit the established library appearance, focus and motion conventions.
